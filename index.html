THE RULEBOOK

How to play.

  • Roll a six to bring a token out of its yard.
  • Move clockwise, then up your colored home lane.
  • Land on an opponent to send them to their yard, unless the square is safe.
  • Reach home with an exact roll. A six or capture earns another turn.
  • First player to bring all four tokens home wins.
